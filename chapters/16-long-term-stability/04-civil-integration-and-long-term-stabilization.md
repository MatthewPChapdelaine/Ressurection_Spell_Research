**3.1 System-Wide Black Box Architecture and Modular Mechanics**

The operational structure of the **Anastasis working** relies on a highly modular design divided into nine distinct functional units known as **black box subsystems**. In magical engineering, a black box represents an isolated operational component whose internal complexity is completely encapsulated. The practitioner does not attempt to solve or prove the inner metaphysical mechanics of the component during execution; instead, the practitioner interacts strictly with the subsystem through five standardized interface specifications: the mandatory **input stream**, the expected **output payload**, the unbreakable **operational invariant**, the identifiable **failure signature**, and the unproven **open proof (OP)**. By strictly standardizing these interfaces across all nine modules, the ritual achieves exceptional safety and structural reliability, guaranteeing that as long as every open proof holds, the entire complex working will execute without unexpected metaphysical instability.

The primary responsibility of the **Address subsystem (BB-1)** is locating and isolating the target Form within Platonic space without utilizing vague physical names. The required **input** consists of seven spoken differential statements recited in precise numerical sequence during the drawing of the inner kekkai lattice. The expected **output** is an active geometric lattice inscribed on the inner containment shell that locks onto the target Form within its nearest-neighbor spatial margin. The foundational **invariant** dictates that every differential must express a strict conceptual relation rather than a physical or descriptive property. The primary **failure mode** is near-neighbor capture (F1), wherein an incorrect or adjacent Form is locked instead of the target. The carried **open proof (OP-1)** assumes that seven distinct non-descriptor relations are mathematically sufficient to achieve complete individual uniqueness in abstract space without ambiguity.

**3.3 Subsystem Contract BB-2: Gravity (Tuner)**
