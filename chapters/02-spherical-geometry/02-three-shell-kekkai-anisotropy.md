# 2.2 Three-Shell Kekkai Anisotropy

Containment during the Anastasis working relies on three nested, concentric spherical boundaries known as **kekkai shells**. Crucially, these walls are entirely non-isotropic; their permeability properties are highly directional and selective, configured to enforce distinct physical and metaphysical laws across each boundary layer. This step-down architecture ensures that abstract information can enter the working volume without prematurely interacting with uncontrolled physical matter.

The innermost barrier, the **Inner Wall**, operates as a selective metaphysical filter. It allows abstract Forms to pass inward while completely blocking physical matter from entering the core volume. This wall isolates what is known as the **half-index state**—a specialized domain existing between pure conceptual space and concrete physical reality. Within this zone, an incoming Form can exist without experiencing immediate dissipation, providing a stable intermediate environment where abstract patterns can be safely held.

Surrounding the inner boundary is the **Mid Wall**, which defines the primary **print volume**. The Mid Wall is entirely impermeable to all external elements, maintaining a completely dry, isolated interior space. It is within this controlled environment that the physical soma is constructed layer by layer. By holding the occupant in a hybrid state—neither fully abstract nor fully physical—the Mid Wall allows the printer and binder mechanics to systematically reconstruct organic tissues while preserving the structural integrity of the target Form.

The outermost boundary, the **Outer Wall**, provides absolute containment against the external environment. It prevents any matter, energy, or metaphysical noise from crossing into or escaping out of the working sphere. However, the Outer Wall features a specialized structural exception: it permits the caster's continuous **anchor draw** to pass directly through its barrier. This unique permeability allows the caster to supply vital persistence energy to the interior without breaching the environmental seal.
