While the initial seven verses act strictly as directional coordinates, the final vocalization consists of a single imperative operational command: *"Close."*

The execution of the *"Close"* command triggers Subsystem BB-8 to seal the final dermal seam, binding the held pattern into the physical body. If the caster hesitates or fails to vocalize the closure command rapidly, the seam stalls, causing ghost gap escape (F7). Vocalizing the command firmly and without delay ensures instant structural integration, completing the transition from an abstract Form into a fully resident physical being.
