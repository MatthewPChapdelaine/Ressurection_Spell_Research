# 8.2 Exhaustive Analysis of the Seven Differential Verses

The recitation chant comprises seven distinct verses, each corresponding to one of the seven mandatory differential relations required by Subsystem Contract BB-1. The first verse addresses **Negative Individuation** (*"Not the one they were mistaken for"*). This statement isolates the target Form by explicitly carving away its nearest abstract sibling. By declaring what the subject was constantly mistaken for in life, the caster eliminates adjacent coordinate collisions, establishing a clear boundary around the target pattern before initiating deeper retrieval.

The second verse defines the **Formative Parent** relation (*"Out of the one they came from"*). This verse anchors the search vector against the dominant conceptual lineage from which the individual's identity emerged. The third verse establishes the **Lattice Curvature Match** (*"By the curve the body still holds"*), reading the residual geometric shape left within the subject's biological remains. This step utilizes the physical corpse strictly as a tuning reference, aligning the abstract search grid with the specific structural frequency of the deceased body.

The fourth verse articulates the **Counter-Form of Chief Privation** (*"Against the shape of what they never had"*). Following classical Platonic theory, an entity's identity is defined as sharply by its fundamental absences as by its presence. Identifying the target's core life privation provides an irreplaceable negative coordinate. The fifth verse incorporates **The Form of Their Own Death** (*"Through the door they already went out of"*). This verse locks the retrieval to the exact moment of biological link loss, ensuring that the spell fetches the final, complete version of the individual rather than an incomplete earlier instance.

The sixth verse introduces the **Witness Distance** relation (*"At this distance from the one still standing"*), spoken while pointing directly to the required witness standing outside the outer kekkai boundary. The witness acts as a fixed physical reference post, providing a live relational anchor that stabilizes the incoming pull vector. Finally, the seventh verse delivers the **Self-Differential** (*"And by the way they stood toward being here at all"*). This crucial final line establishes the target's own attitude toward physical existence, completing the seven-vertex closure and pinpointing the exact coordinate in Platonic space.
