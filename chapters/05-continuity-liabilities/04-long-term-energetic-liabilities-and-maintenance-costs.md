# 5.4 Long-Term Energetic Liabilities and Maintenance Costs

Beyond the immediate execution window, the Anastasis working imposes ongoing energetic liabilities on the surrounding environment. Because a reinstated entity remains anchored by artificial persistence until natural organic homeostasis fully stabilizes, the physical location where the working occurred retains a residual metric distortion. This residual warp creates a minor but persistent energetic drain on local ambient fields, requiring regular environmental monitoring to prevent localized spatial shear or metric decay.

Furthermore, the persistent link between the printed soma and its Platonic origin requires ongoing maintenance during the post-instantiation integration phase. If the initial persistence debt settlement at Phase P9 was incomplete or unevenly distributed, the returned individual may experience gradual dermal seam erosion or minor structural instability over extended periods. Ensuring long-term continuity requires the practitioner to maintain continuous balance across all subsystem contracts, guaranteeing that the systemic costs of the working are fully accounted for without compromising the stability of either the caster or the returned subject.
