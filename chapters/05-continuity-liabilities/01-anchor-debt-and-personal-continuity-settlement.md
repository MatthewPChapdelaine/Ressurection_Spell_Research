# 5.1 Anchor Debt and Personal Continuity Settlement

The primary systemic price of the **Anastasis working** is paid through Subsystem Contract BB-9 during the settlement phase at Phase P9. Because Platonic Forms do not possess natural physical endurance, the spell must supply artificial persistence across all containment boundaries throughout the retrieval, printing, and binding sequences. This required energy cannot be drawn from physical life force or ambient mana without disrupting the half-index state. Instead, the debt is extracted directly from the primary caster's personal time continuity. Under standard operational parameters, a single full execution consumes thirty to forty minutes of subjective continuity, which manifests after settlement as a distinct, unrecoverable blank patch in the caster's temporal awareness.

Attempting multiple workings within a short temporal window drastically escalates the continuity liability. Performing a second working within thirty calendar days results in an exponential multiplier, doubling the extracted debt to over eighty minutes of subjective continuity. This compounding draw creates severe psychological stress, as the caster's personal timeline suffers acute fragmentation. If the persistence debt exceeds the caster's available continuity reserves prior to Phase P9, Subsystem BB-9 experiences anchor overrun (F8). In this catastrophic state, the caster stops existing as an independent observer and becomes permanently fused into the target Form's relational coordinate network.
