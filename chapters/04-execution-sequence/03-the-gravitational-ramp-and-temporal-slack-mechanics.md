# 4.3 The Gravitational Ramp and Temporal Slack Mechanics

The central dynamic of the working occurs during **Phase P4 (6:00)**, which governs the gravitational retrieval ramp. This phase contains the only operational **temporal slack** in the entire ritual sequence. While standard phases operate under rigid duration limits, Phase P4 permits controlled time expansion to accommodate larger spatial volumes. For a nominal human radius **R** of 1.9 meters, the gravitational ramp requires a base acceleration time of ninety seconds. However, if the target silhouette requires a larger containment radius, the caster must extend the ramp duration by adding twenty seconds for every additional 0.3 meters of radius clearance. This smooth acceleration curve prevents sudden force steps that would otherwise cause catastrophic shear collapse (F2) along the retrieval axis.
