# 4.1 Master Timeline and Structural Rigidity

The execution of the **Anastasis working** follows a strict temporal timeline totaling precisely eleven minutes and twenty seconds (11:20). Every phase within the ritual sequence operates under rigid time floors rather than flexible targets. These time floors establish the absolute minimum duration required for each underlying metaphysical process to settle safely within the spherical containment field. Because abstract Forms lack natural physical endurance, rushing any individual phase risks severe spatial distortion or catastrophic structural collapse. Consequently, the caster must maintain continuous temporal discipline throughout the operational window, advancing through phases P0 through P9 only when the mandatory minimum time requirements have been completely fulfilled.
