# 7.1 Floor Face Kai Activation and Perimeter Clearance

The completion of seam binding at Phase P7 marks the transition of the target Form from a held abstract pattern to a resident physical entity. However, the newly instantiated occupant remains isolated within the three-shell kekkai architecture until Phase P8 (11:00). Releasing the occupant requires precise operational execution to prevent atmospheric disturbance or sudden pressure drops across the containment volume. Rather than dropping all containment barriers simultaneously, the primary caster must execute a selective **kai activation** targeting strictly the floor face of the inner, mid, and outer kekkai walls.

Opening the floor face allows the subject to step naturally outward onto the physical floor while the vertical sidewalls and upper dome of the kekkai remain fully active. This partial opening preserves structural field stability, preventing ambient environmental noise or uncontrolled thermal currents from rushing into the core print volume. The occupant must step forward until their physical body clears the outer wall perimeter by at least one full body length. Only after the witness confirms total physical clearance may the caster systematically lower the remaining outer, mid, and inner walls in reverse order during Phase P9.
