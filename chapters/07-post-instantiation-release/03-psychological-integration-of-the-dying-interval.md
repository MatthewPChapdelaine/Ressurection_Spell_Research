# 7.3 Psychological Integration of the Dying Interval

A fundamental psychological reality of the Anastasis working stems from Core Doctrine D1 and Differential Relation D5. Because the target Form is retrieved by establishing an address coordinate tied to the exact shape of their death, the returned entity arrives with their memory of dying completely intact. The subject experiences no amnesia regarding the final moments of biological link loss. Furthermore, while external observers experience the post-mortem period as a prolonged temporal duration, the returned entity perceives the entire interval between death and instantiation as an instantaneous gap.

Practitioners and attending witnesses are strictly forbidden from pre-explaining or attempting to soften the memory of the dying interval. Attempting to narrate or reframe the event before the subject has fully integrated their returned state introduces severe cognitive dissonance and memory distortion. The individual must be granted time to process the sudden temporal transition naturally. Respecting this cognitive boundary ensures smooth psychological reconciliation, allowing the returned person to reconcile their past memories with their current physical reality without triggering systemic panic or identity fracture.
