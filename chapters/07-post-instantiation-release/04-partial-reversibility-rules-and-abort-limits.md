# 7.4 Partial Reversibility Rules and Abort Limits

The operational sequence of the Anastasis spell contains strict rules governing cancellation and reversibility. Prior to Phase P5, the retrieval process remains completely reversible. If a diagnostic signature indicates an address mismatch (F1) or gravitational shear (F2) before soma printing begins, the caster can safely halt the working. Releasing the unanchored pattern at this early stage allows the Form to slip back into Platonic space without incurring structural debt or physical harm, permitting the caster to re-evaluate parameters and re-attempt the working at a later time.

Once soma printing commences at Phase P6, partial reversibility is permanently lost. If a critical failure occurs during tissue synthesis or seam binding, the caster cannot simply reset the operation back to Phase P5. Interrupting an active soma print results in immediate structural collapse, destroying the partially printed body and releasing an incomplete, corrupted pattern. In the event of an unrecoverable late-stage failure, the practitioner must execute a full system abort, clearing the entire working volume and repeating the complete eleven-minute ritual sequence from Phase P0.
