# 3.2 Subsystem Contract BB-1: Address (Triangulator)

The primary responsibility of the **Address subsystem (BB-1)** is locating and isolating the target Form within Platonic space without utilizing vague physical names. The required **input** consists of seven spoken differential statements recited in precise numerical sequence during the drawing of the inner kekkai lattice. The expected **output** is an active geometric lattice inscribed on the inner containment shell that locks onto the target Form within its nearest-neighbor spatial margin. The foundational **invariant** dictates that every differential must express a strict conceptual relation rather than a physical or descriptive property. The primary **failure mode** is near-neighbor capture (F1), wherein an incorrect or adjacent Form is locked instead of the target. The carried **open proof (OP-1)** assumes that seven distinct non-descriptor relations are mathematically sufficient to achieve complete individual uniqueness in abstract space without ambiguity.
