# 3.6 Subsystem Contract BB-5: Link (Position Handshake)

The **Link subsystem (BB-5)** establishes the active connection between the retrieved abstract pattern and the physical printing volume. The subsystem accepts the held target Form at the singularity knot as its **input**. It produces as **output** a bi-directional position handshake that locks the abstract coordinate directly to the physical print volume. The strict **invariant** requires the link to operate as a direct spatial channel rather than creating a duplicate or cloned copy of the original Form. Its primary **failure mode** is double occupancy (F5), occurring when the Platonic origin fails to release its hold, resulting in two co-existing instances. The corresponding **open proof (OP-5)** assumes that the position handshake couples directly with the internal structure of the target Form rather than binding merely to the artificial singularity knot.
