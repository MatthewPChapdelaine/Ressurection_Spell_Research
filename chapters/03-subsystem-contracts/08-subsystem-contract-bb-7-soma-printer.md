# 3.8 Subsystem Contract BB-7: Soma (Printer)

The **Soma subsystem (BB-7)** executes the physical construction of the organic body inside the mid kekkai shell. Its required **inputs** include the RSI surface geometry, objective Form invariants, and a physical substrate seed. The expected **output** is a fully constructed human body built inside-out, starting with deep viscera and finishing at the skin layer. The mandatory **invariant** dictates that interior internal organ systems derive strictly from Form invariants, while surface contours derive from RSI data, reconciling the two at the dermis. If printing fails, it manifests as **structural deformation (F4)** during skin assembly. The crucial **open proof (OP-7)** assumes that the boundary reconciliation between internal Form architecture and external RSI surface geometry is mathematically convergent across all ten printing heads on the mid wall.
