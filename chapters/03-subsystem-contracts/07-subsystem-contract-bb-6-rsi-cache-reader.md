# 3.7 Subsystem Contract BB-6: RSI (Cache Reader)

The **Residual Self-Image subsystem (BB-6)** extracts surface geometry and external appearance data from memory residue. It takes as **input** the target's subjective residual self-image held within biological remains or witness memory. The system outputs **detailed topological surface metrics**, including external silhouette shapes, proportional bias, hand geometry, and remembered clothing. The operational **invariant** recognizes that RSI data is inherently lossy and subjective, providing surface mapping without supplying valid internal organ structures. The subsystem's main **failure mode** is the self-portrait body (F4), where subjective memory overwrites structural truth, producing an idealized but unviable physical body. The carried **open proof (OP-6)** assumes that the objective Form invariants will overwrite subjective RSI surface errors faster than RSI errors can distort the underlying Form structure.
