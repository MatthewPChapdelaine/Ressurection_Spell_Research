# 3.9 Subsystem Contract BB-8: Binder (Seam)

The **Binder subsystem (BB-8)** performs the final integration between the printed physical body and the held abstract Form. Its **inputs** consist of the completed physical soma and the stabilized pattern held at the singularity knot. The system yields as **output** a fully integrated living entity, sewn securely into the body at the final dermal seam. The vital **invariant** mandates absolute seam closure before releasing containment, turning a temporary held pattern into a permanent resident form. The catastrophic **failure signature** is the ghost gap (F7), where a delayed seam allows the pattern to escape, leaving behind an empty, unviable shell. The corresponding **open proof (OP-8)** assumes that the physical seam binding process can complete entirely in under four seconds without incurring structural friction.
