# 3.5 Subsystem Contract BB-4: Kekkai (Isolator)

The **Kekkai subsystem (BB-4)** manages the three nested spherical barriers that establish environmental control over the working volume. Its primary **inputs** are directional activation commands: jou (reinforce boundary), kai (open selective face), and ketsu (annihilate interior). The system generates as **output** a three-layer anisotropic containment field isolating the half-index state. The absolute **invariant** requires all three shells to be completely raised before pulling begins, ensuring the outer shell is never breached while the interior remains occupied. The system's catastrophic **failure signature** is inversion (F6), caused by firing ketsu while the center is filled, destroying both sphere and occupant. The associated **open proof (OP-4)** assumes that non-isotropic walls can hold a half-index state without accidentally registering or indexing the pattern themselves.
