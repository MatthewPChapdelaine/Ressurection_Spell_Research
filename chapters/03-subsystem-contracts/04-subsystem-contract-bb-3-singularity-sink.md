# 3.4 Subsystem Contract BB-3: Singularity (Sink)

The **Singularity subsystem (BB-3)** acts as the focal reception point at the geometric center of the containment volume. It takes as **input** the tuned gravitational vector generated by BB-2. The resulting **output** is a highly stabilized target Form held motionless at the singularity knot prior to body printing. The core **invariant** specifies that the singularity knot is the only construct capable of holding an unanchored abstract Form without requiring a physical living substrate. The characteristic **failure mode** is overdraw bleed (F3), where the knot overheating forces the system to consume its own address coordinates to maintain stability. The corresponding **open proof (OP-3)** assumes that the singularity knot can hold the complex Form indefinitely without corrupting or eroding its underlying Platonic address.
