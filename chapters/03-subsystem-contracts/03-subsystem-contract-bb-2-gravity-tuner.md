# 3.3 Subsystem Contract BB-2: Gravity (Tuner)

The **Gravity subsystem (BB-2)** functions as the primary pulling mechanism that draws the isolated Form toward the working volume. The system accepts as **input** the calculated address density value (δ) and a smooth, continuous mathematical ramp curve. The system yields as **output** an asymptotic, saturating gravitational pull centered directly on the singularity knot. The non-negotiable **invariant** states that the gravitational acceleration must never step abruptly or increase instantly, as sudden speed shifts cause catastrophic metric shearing. If the system fails, it manifests as **shear collapse (F2)**, dragging a torn pattern along the ramp axis. The underlying **open proof (OP-2)** assumes that the asymptotic force curve saturates safely below the elastic stress limit of the inner kekkai wall throughout the entire pulling sequence.
