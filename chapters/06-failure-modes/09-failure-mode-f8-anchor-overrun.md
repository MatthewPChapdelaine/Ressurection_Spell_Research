# 6.9 Failure Mode F8: Anchor Overrun

The eighth failure mode, **F8 (Anchor Overrun)**, occurs in Subsystem BB-9 when the primary caster's personal time continuity reserves are exhausted before Phase P9 settlement. The diagnostic signature is recognized by the caster experiencing severe identity distortion, auditory hallucinations, and a blurring of personal memories with those of the returned entity. Visually, the caster's anchor draw lines on the outer wall begin to flicker and bleed into the interior volume.

The pre-committed interlock for F8 requires a designated secondary caster standing on standby from Phase P6 onward. If the primary caster shows signs of continuity exhaustion or anchor overrun, the secondary caster immediately steps into the draw line, supplying backup continuity to settle the persistence debt safely without compromising the primary caster's mental integrity.
