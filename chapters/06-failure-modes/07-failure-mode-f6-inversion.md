# 6.7 Failure Mode F6: Inversion

The sixth failure mode, **F6 (Inversion)**, is the most destructive operational error in the Anastasis working, resulting from improper command execution in Subsystem BB-4. Inversion occurs if the annihilation command (ketsu) is accidentally triggered while the interior volume is occupied by a retrieved Form. The signature is instantaneous: a flash of dark implosive energy consumes the entire containment sphere, cleanly collapsing both the kekkai boundaries and the interior occupant into a spatial point, leaving zero physical or metaphysical residue behind.

The interlock for F6 is strict and absolute: the command ketsu is completely locked out of the system's operational control structure from Phase P5 through Phase P9. By establishing an unbreakable safety lockout, the working eliminates the possibility of accidental interior annihilation during active binding.
