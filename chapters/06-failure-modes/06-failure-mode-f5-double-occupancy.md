# 6.6 Failure Mode F5: Double Occupancy

The fifth failure mode, **F5 (Double Occupancy)**, arises within Subsystem BB-5 when the position handshake fails to completely release the target pattern from its Platonic origin point before seam closure. The diagnostic signature manifests at Phase P7 as a brief, translucent double image or shadow oscillating around the physical soma. This optical aberration indicates that two active instances of the same Form are momentarily co-existing across physical and metaphysical domains, creating severe structural tension along the position channel.

The interlock for F5 demands absolute verification of the bi-directional position link prior to seam binding. Under no circumstances may the caster execute the final binder closure against an unstruck or dual-occupied link. The caster must delay seam execution until the original Platonic coordinate fully releases its hold, ensuring single occupancy within the print volume.
