# 1.2 Core Doctrine 1: Death as Link Loss, Not Erasure

The first foundational claim of Anastasis (D1) establishes that biological death represents a severed communication link rather than the destruction of the person. When a biological entity dies, the physical body suffers structural failure, causing the position handshake between the physical substrate and the individual's eternal Form in Platonic space to drop. The Form itself is not evicted, harmed, or erased from the metaphysical environment. Consequently, the Anastasis spell is strictly an information *fetch* operation rather than a reconstruction or repair process. The physical corpse left behind serves merely as a tuning fork—a residual artifact holding geometric curvature that allows the practitioner to locate the original address in Platonic space before stepping away completely.
