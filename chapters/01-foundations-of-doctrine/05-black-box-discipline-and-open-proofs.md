# 1.5 Black Box Discipline and Open Proofs

To maintain safety and execution rigor during a complex ninth-rank ritual, the Anastasis working relies on Black Box Discipline. The system breaks down into nine modular subsystems, each treated as a formal black box defined by five essential components: **input, output, invariant, failure signature,** and an **open proof (OP)**. An open proof represents an unproven mathematical or metaphysical assumption that the caster must carry without solving directly. The entire working is structured so that if all nine open proofs hold, the operation executes with absolute correctness. Furthermore, the operational sequence is intentionally designed to trigger the cheapest, most likely open proof failures during the earliest phases of execution, preventing catastrophic resource loss or physical danger.

\---

\#\# 0\. Doctrine

The working rests on three claims. Every box below descends from them, so state them plainly.

**D1. Death is link loss, not erasure.** You already have this. The body's failure severed the position handshake to the person's address in Platonic space; it did not evict the Form. So this is a *fetch*, not a repair. Nothing is resurrected — something is *re-instantiated*, and the corpse is left holding a shadow you can tune against and then walk away from.

**D2. A Form has no persistence of its own.** Persistence is a physical property, and Forms are not physical. The instant a pattern arrives it begins to slip, because nothing outside Platonic space is holding it. This is the real problem the spell solves, and it is why the kekkai is load-bearing rather than decorative: the working must supply persistence artificially and continuously, from arrival to closure, and stop supplying it exactly when the binding is complete. A sphere that opens early doesn't leak a person — it leaks a *description* of one.

**D3. A Form cannot be named.** Names are physical-language handles with terrible collision rates. You triangulate instead, by differential, the way you'd fix a point in a dark room by touching three walls.

Your design already has the right instinct in the two places it matters: the body is irrelevant as material but valuable as *residue*, and the residual self-image supplies clothing. Both are used below, in those roles only.

\---

\#\# 1\. Black Box discipline

In this working a black box is a formal stub with five declared parts: **input, output, invariant, failure signature, open proof (OP).** The OP is the thing the caster does not discharge, only assumes and carries. Nine boxes means nine carried proofs and a working that is *correct if they hold*.

That is the honest status of the spell. It is not a proof. It is a well-typed assumption set with a sequence attached, and the sequence is arranged so that the most likely OPs to fail fail *cheaply* and *early*.
