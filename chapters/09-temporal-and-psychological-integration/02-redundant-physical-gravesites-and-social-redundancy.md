# 9.2 Redundant Physical Gravesites and Social Redundancy

A prominent social friction point following post-instantiation release is the physical reality of the redundant gravesite. Because the Anastasis framework utilizes biological remains strictly as a geometric residue to derive curvature before stepping away, the original physical corpse or interred ashes remain in their resting place. The returned individual is forced to exist alongside their own official burial site, memorial markers, and public obituaries. This physical redundancy creates acute psychological dissonance, as the returned person confronts concrete societal proof of their own concluded life.

This material redundancy extends directly into the social sphere, generating severe organizational and interpersonal complications. Legal contracts, marital bonds, property inheritances, and professional roles are universally dissolved or reassigned upon biological death. The sudden return of an individual possessing verified Platonic identity disrupt established estate settlements and social structures. Surrounding community members frequently struggle to reconcile their completed mourning processes with the living presence of the deceased, leading to social alienation, emotional paralysis, and institutional resistance.
