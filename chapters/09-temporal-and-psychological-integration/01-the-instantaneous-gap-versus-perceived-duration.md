# 9.1 The Instantaneous Gap versus Perceived Duration

The central psychological anomaly of the **Anastasis working** arises from the fundamental difference in how time is experienced by the returned entity versus external observers. According to Core Doctrine D1, biological death represents a severed communication link rather than the destruction or pause of consciousness. When the position handshake drops, the subject's Form remains static in Platonic space, completely outside the physical progression of time. Consequently, upon reinstatement at Phase P8, the returned individual experiences zero subjective passage of time between the instant of link loss and the moment of physical awakening. To the target Form, the transition feels entirely instantaneous—a single breath interrupted and immediately resumed.

Conversely, external observers experience the post-mortem interval as a linear, unrelenting duration filled with grief, societal adjustment, and environmental decay. This temporal mismatch creates immediate cognitive friction upon release. While surviving friends, family, and associates have processed months or years of emotional recovery and structural change, the instantiated entity retains the fresh emotional state, acute memories, and immediate expectations of the exact moment they died. The returned individual wakes expecting zero change in their social circle, only to encounter a world that has moved forward without them.
