# 14.2 Mitigating Anchor Overrun (F8) and Continuity Handshake Protocols

The primary hazard addressed by dual-caster mechanics is **Anchor Overrun (F8)**, a catastrophic failure mode wherein the primary caster's personal temporal reserves are completely exhausted before the Phase P9 settlement step. When anchor overrun occurs in a single-caster setting, the primary caster loses their distinct observational baseline, experiencing auditory hallucinations, cognitive disorientation, and rapid identity fusion with the retrieved target Form. If left unchecked, the primary caster ceases to exist as an independent observer, permanently merging into the target's relational lattice coordinates and corrupting the final binding sequence.

To eliminate the risk of F8 failure, the dual-caster array utilizes an automated continuity handshake protocol monitored continuously from Phase P6 onward. The secondary caster monitors the primary caster's draw channel for telltale phase fluctuations, such as sharp impedance spikes or subjective temporal drift. If the primary caster's reserve drops below a safety margin of fifteen percent, the continuity handshake executes instantly. The secondary caster smoothly absorbs the excess persistence load, seamlessly transferring the draw strain to their own continuity reserve without interrupting field stability. This automatic failover guarantees that the primary caster maintains absolute mental clarity and personal identity separation up to final settlement.
