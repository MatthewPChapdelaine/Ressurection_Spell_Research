# 15.3 Estate Dissolution, Contractual Termination, and Civil Reinstatement

Upon biological death, an individual's estate undergoes immediate legal probate, distributing property, settling liabilities, and dissolving existing contracts. Reinstating an instantiated person does not void these legal settlements. Property transferred to heirs or creditors remains legally theirs, and marital or parental obligations are permanently concluded under statutory law. Rather than attempting to overturn completed probate actions, practitioners must guide the returned individual through establishing an entirely new legal identity. Navigating civil administrative channels as a newly registered entity ensures social integration without exposing the confidential mechanics of the Anastasis working.
