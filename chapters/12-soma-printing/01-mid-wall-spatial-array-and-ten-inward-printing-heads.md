# 12.1 Mid-Wall Spatial Array and Ten Inward Printing Heads

The physical synthesis of organic tissue within the **Anastasis working** is governed by Subsystem Contract BB-7 across Phase P6. This process takes place exclusively inside the dry print volume enclosed by the mid kekkai wall. Mounted directly onto the interior surface of this mid boundary is a specialized spatial array consisting of **ten inward printing heads**. These heads do not deposit external biological materials; instead, they project focused streams of dense physical substrate energy inward toward the central singularity knot, materializing cellular structures directly out of the half-index state.

The ten projection heads are arranged in a symmetric decahedral geometry, ensuring complete spatial coverage across all three dimensions of the target pattern. Each head maintains real-time alignment with the central focal vector, adjusting its emission frequency to match the local structural requirements of the growing soma. Because the interior environment remains completely isolated from external matter, the ten heads operate in absolute unison, continuously balancing projection intensity to prevent structural voids or uneven density gradients within the emerging physical body.
