**1.1 Introduction to the Anastasis Framework**

The Anastasis working is a ninth-rank ritual system designed for ontological retrieval and form-instantiation. The first foundational claim of Anastasis (D1) establishes that biological death represents a severed communication link rather than the destruction of the person. When a biological entity dies, the physical body suffers structural failure, causing the position handshake between the physical substrate and the individual's eternal Form in Platonic space to drop. Consequently, the Anastasis spell is strictly an information *fetch* operation rather than a reconstruction or repair process. The physical corpse left behind serves merely as a tuning fork—a residual artifact holding geometric curvature that allows the practitioner to locate the original address in Platonic space before stepping away completely.

The second claim (D2) addresses the fundamental instability of retrieved Forms outside their native domain. The moment an abstract pattern is retrieved into the physical realm, it immediately begins to degrade and slip unless artificially anchored. This reality makes the spherical kekkai boundary an essential structural component rather than a decorative ward. The kekkai must supply continuous, artificial persistence across three anisotropic shells throughout the retrieval, printing, and binding phases. If the outer containment field collapses prematurely, the system does not release a living person; instead, it dissipates an abstract *description* into the surrounding environment.

**1.4 Core Doctrine 3: Differential Triangulation**

The third claim (D3) governs how a Form is specified during retrieval. Abstract Forms cannot be summoned using standard linguistic names, as physical language carries high collision rates and imprecise definitions. Instead, the practitioner must locate the target Form through differential triangulation. By defining a set of seven precise, non-descriptor relations—such as what the individual was constantly mistaken for, their formative concept-parents, and the specific shape of their death—the practitioner isolates the target coordinate within Platonic space. **1.5 Black Box Discipline and Open Proofs**

To maintain safety and execution rigor during a complex ninth-rank ritual, the Anastasis working relies on Black Box Discipline.
